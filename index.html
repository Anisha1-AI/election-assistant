Registration Guide

How to Register Online

  1. 1 Visit the official National Voters' Services Portal (NVSP).
  2. 2 Click on "Fill Form 6" (For First Time Voters).
  3. 3 Enter your details and upload the required documents.
  4. 4 Submit and note down your Reference ID to track status.
Go to Official Registration Website

Required Documents

  • Recent Passport Size Photograph
  • Age Proof Birth Certificate, 10th Marksheet, Aadhaar
  • Address Proof Passport, Utility Bill, Aadhaar

The Voting Process

1

Visit Booth

Go to your designated polling booth. Check your name in the voter list.

2

ID Verification

Show your Voter ID or alternative approved ID to the polling officer.

3

Ink Marking

An official will mark your index finger with indelible ink and take your signature.

4

Cast Vote

Press the blue button next to your candidate on the EVM. Check the VVPAT slip.

Polling Station

Enter your area to find nearby polling locations on the map.

Tip: You can find your exact polling station name on your voter slip.
